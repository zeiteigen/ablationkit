# Acme support FAQ

Refunds are issued within 5 business days after the returned item arrives. Contact support for faster refunds.

Shipping is free for orders over 50 dollars. Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 days.

Passwords can be reset from the login page with the forgot password link. The reset email expires after one hour.

Order status is shown on the orders page. The lookup_order tool returns the live status of an order.

Warranty covers manufacturing defects for two years. Accidental damage is not covered by the warranty.

Support is open Monday to Friday from 9 to 17. Weekend requests are answered on Monday.
